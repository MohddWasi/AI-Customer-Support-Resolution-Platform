# Support FAQ

## Can the AI answer questions without documentation?

It can attempt classification and investigation, but it should not present unsupported product behavior as fact.

## Can the AI change a customer's account?

Only through explicitly authorized tools and the required approval workflow.

## What happens when documentation is insufficient?

The AI should ask a focused clarification question or escalate the ticket.

## Why search historical tickets?

A previous ticket can provide useful resolution patterns, but historical tickets are not automatically authoritative. Product documentation and approved policies take precedence when they conflict with a historical ticket.

## Why are citations required?

Citations let the support agent inspect the evidence behind an AI response and make hallucinated claims easier to detect.
