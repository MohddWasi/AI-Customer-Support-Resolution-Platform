# Ticket Handling Workflow

1. Receive ticket.
2. Identify category and intent.
3. Determine priority.
4. Retrieve relevant product documentation.
5. Search historical resolved tickets.
6. Determine whether a tool is needed.
7. Execute only authorized tools.
8. Generate a proposed response.
9. Verify factual support and citations.
10. Escalate or request human approval when required.
11. Send the final response.
12. Store the AI run, evidence, tool calls, and outcome for audit/evaluation.
